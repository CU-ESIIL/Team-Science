## Team Science Knowledge

Many of your team science skills you can only hone through practice (*and failure!*) While we provide a vareity of explainers to help teams and those non affiliated with ESIIL learn some tools and skills to be better collaborators, understanding why we focus on these skills and tools is important. 

For those interested in team science from a **science** and **education** perspective, we have put together a couple of key resource documents. These are available both on our [Zenodo ESIIL Community](https://zenodo.org/communities/esiil/records?q=&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=newest) and our [ESIIL SciTS webpage](https://esiil.org/science-team-science). Please excuse our dust as we iron out the tagging system on our website!

For those interested in advancing your team science knowledge independently we recommend checking out our resources on: 

- [Team science books and reports](https://zenodo.org/records/20617745)
- [Toolkits and guides](https://zenodo.org/records/20512820)

For those interested in taking a deeper dive and looking at some available free and low-cost trainings to advance your professional development (not affiliated with nor sponsored by ESIIL), check out our resource on: 

- [Online courses and trainings](https://zenodo.org/records/20617853)
